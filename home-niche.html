Crew Culture

Build Individual Swagger and Tight Team Coordination

We focus on street culture principles, rhythm counts, and improvisation freedom (freestyling) to help kids express their personality and sync in crews.

Popping & Locking

Joint isolations, body hits, hand locks, and rhythmic beat accents.

Breaking Floorworks

Safe footwork grids, baby freezes, spins, and balance holds.

Our Specialties

Core Styles We Teach

Discover the diverse branches of street dance that make up our Urban Beat curriculum.

01

Breaking

Toprocks, downrocks, power moves, and freezes. Perfect for building agility, upper body strength, and stamina.

02

Popping & Waves

Learn muscle isolation, robotic animations, and body waves. Excellent for developing fine motor control and timing.

03

Locking

Fast hand rolls, wrist snaps, pointing, and dramatic locks. Builds coordination, energy, and fun stage character.

04

Crew Battles

Synchronized group choreography and friendly battle mockups. Inspires teamwork, respect, and confidence.

Our Methodology

Why TwinkleArts Urban Beat?

We balance the raw street roots of hip-hop with structured training to give kids the ultimate personal development outlet.

✔
Improvisation & Freestyle Cyphers

We don't just teach memory-based choreography; we teach kids how to listen to the beat and express their own style freely in cypher circles.

✔
Safety-First Gymnastics & Freezes

All ground freezes, power moves, and balances are instructed on safety mats under the careful guidance of certified instructors.

✔
Confidence-Building Performances

Through team faceoffs and battle cyphers, we encourage children to step outside their comfort zones, building long-term stage swagger.
