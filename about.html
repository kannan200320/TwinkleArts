Our Legacy

Our Journey Through Time

2012

The Founding

TwinkleArts was founded with a single studio room, two instructors, and 15 students taking classical ballet classes.

2015

Expansion of Styles

Introduced new vocal music, acting drama, and Indian Bharatnatyam classical training disciplines.

2018

First Theatre Recital

Hosted our first annual performing arts theater recital at the City Playhouse with 100+ children performing on stage.

2021

New State-of-the-Art HQ

Moved to our modern headquarters with 4 large acoustic studios, shock-absorbent wooden floors, parent viewing lounges, and digital enrollment systems.

2026

Interactive Parent Portal

Launched the custom kids dashboard attendance, billing invoices, batch registration grids, and sibling discounts.

Our Environment

State-of-the-Art Facilities

We believe that a professional, safe, and welcoming environment is key to nurturing young performers.

Harlequin Floors

Professional shock-absorbent sprung wooden floors designed to protect young dancers' joints, ankles, and spine during leaps and high-impact routines.

Acoustic Soundproofing

Acoustically isolated studios featuring state-of-the-art surround sound systems, custom speakers, and professional soundproofing to block crossover noise.

Parent Viewing Lobbies

Comfortable, spacious waiting lounges with high-speed Wi-Fi, study desks, and flat-screen monitors streaming live CCTV feeds of the studio classes.

Private Dressing Rooms

Clean and secure dressing spaces equipped with makeup mirrors, hanger stations, and personal lockers for easy, stress-free recital costume prep.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Core Values

We are dedicated to fostering a supportive environment where children can thrive artistically, physically, and socially.

Our Mission

To inspire, educate, and empower young minds through the creative freedom and structural discipline of the performing arts. We nurture each student's passion while building technical strength.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant, inclusive artistic community where every child discovers their unique voice, gains physical grace, and develops lifelong self-confidence that shines both on and off the stage.

Our Core Values

At TwinkleArts Academy, our foundation is built upon three core pillars: Excellence in our certified coaching methodologies, Inclusivity to create a warm space for all skill levels, and Holistic Growth to foster poise, confidence, and artistic development in every student.
