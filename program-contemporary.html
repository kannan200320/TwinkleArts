Contemporary Flow

Learn Fluid Motion, Gravity Releases, and Creative Floor Rolls

Our Contemporary Flow class introduces dancers to modern release techniques, contract/extend styles, and floor slides. Students explore self-expression and build spatial awareness through fluid choreography routines.

Curriculum Highlights

Stage 1: Floor & Release basics

Breathing patterns, simple floor slides, safe falls, and weight shifts.

Stage 2: Improvisational Flow

Emotional expression, solo routines, leap sequences, and team stage formations.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is prior ballet training required for contemporary class? +

Prior training is helpful but not strictly required. We customize floor movements to accommodate beginners safely.

2. What safety precautions are taken for floor drops? +

Our studios use high-quality shock-absorbing sprung subfloors to protect kids during slides and roll drops.

3. Do you cover improvisational dance styles? +

Yes, part of our curriculum includes structured improvisation prompts to build confidence and choreography intuition.

4. Are knee pads required for lessons? +

Soft knee pads are highly recommended for advanced floor work batches to avoid mild bruising.
