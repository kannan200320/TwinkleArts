Start Dancing

Your Dance Journey in 3 Steps

Joining TwinkleArts is simple. Here is how we guide your child from their first step to performing on stage.

01

Choose Your Style

Explore our diverse programs online. Select from Classical Ballet, Urban Hip-Hop, Classical Bharatnatyam, Drama, or Vocal classes based on your child's age and interest.

02

Book a Free Trial

Register online for a complimentary trial session. Let your child experience the class environment, meet their teacher, and get a feel for the rhythms and style.

03

Start Performing

Enroll in a weekly batch, track growth on your interactive dashboard, and prepare to showcase your routines at our grand annual theatre recital stage.
